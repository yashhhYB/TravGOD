TRAVGOD

Connecting tourists directly with background-verified local guides to avoid scams, bypass language barriers, and travel safely.

💡 The Unique Kernel

A unified travel safety network where location-based scam alerts and price guides meet language-matched, background-verified local guides for free advice or paid bookings.

👤 Target User

An independent tourist exploring a new city (e.g., Paris) who wants transparent price guidance, protection from tourist traps, and native local support in their preferred languages.

🎯 Working Demo Goal

A sleek 60-second flow: Select Paris → view scams & fair price ranges → filter guides by English + Spanish → click verified guide → initiate chat or PayPal booking.
